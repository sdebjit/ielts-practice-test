Why You Will Not Face Anything Unexpected:

Every question set on this platform is calibrated directly to standard Cambridge IELTS Books 1–19 specifications. 📖 Academic Reading Suite 40 Questions • 60 Mins
  • 3 Academic Passages (~2,200 words) scaled from descriptive (Passage 1) to analytical (Passage 3).
  • All Cambridge Question Formats: True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given, Matching Headings (i–viii), Matching Features, Multiple Choice, and Sentence & Summary Completion.
  • Authentic Raw-to-Band Scale: 39–40 = Band 9.0; 37–38 = Band 8.5; 35–36 = Band 8.0; 30–32 = Band 7.0; 23–26 = Band 6.0.
🎧 Listening Suite 40 Questions • 4 Sections
  • Section 1: Social transactional dialog (forms, names, dates, phone numbers, prices).
  • Section 2: Public orientation monologue (facilities, walking circuits, rules).
  • Section 3: Academic tutorial & laboratory methodology discussion.
  • Section 4: Monologue university scientific lecture (lecture notes completion).
  • Cambridge Distractor Traps: Self-corrections and contradictory distractor phrasing (*"originally thought"*, *"however"*, *"instead"*).
✍️ Writing Evaluation Lab Task 1 & 2 • 60 Mins
  • Task 1 (150+ Words, 20 Mins): Data report summarizing tables, charts, and trends.
  • Task 2 (250+ Words, 40 Mins): Discursive essays (*"Discuss both views"*, *"To what extent do you agree"*, *"Do advantages outweigh disadvantages"*).
  • Official Cambridge 4-Criteria Rubric: Task Achievement, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy.
  • Band 9.0 Exemplars: Authentic model answers with advanced academic collocations.
🗣️ Speaking Simulation 3 Parts • 11–14 Mins
  • Part 1 (4–5 Mins): 4 familiar everyday topic questions.
  • Part 2 (3–4 Mins): Cue Card with 1-min scratchpad notes timer + 2-min uninterrupted speech timer.
  • Part 3 (4–5 Mins): Two-way academic discussion exploring abstract societal/ethical implications.
  • Live Human Voice Examiner: Natural British/Australian speech cadence with filler word tracking.
Cambridge Strictness Marking Protocols:
1. TRUE vs NOT GIVEN Contradiction must be factually disproven to be FALSE. Unmentioned facts must be marked NOT GIVEN.
2. Word Limit Violations Exceeding "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" results in immediate zero marks, exactly as enforced by Cambridge examiners.
3. Spelling & Grammar Both UK and US standard spellings are accepted, but singular/plural precision must match the passage text.
